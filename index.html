Build it. Test it. Then trade it.

Every strategy moves through the same three steps, in the same workspace, on the same data. Scroll through a strategy's life in DheQuest.

1Build

Draw the rules, not the code.

Lay out entries, exits and stops as blocks on a canvas. Combine conditions with AND, OR, ANY and IF branches, and attach actions to buy, sell, modify, square off or alert you.

1Build

Keep every idea, and every version.

Your strategy library holds drafts and finished work side by side. Each save is versioned, so you can compare and roll back.

When a strategy is ready, deploy it and DheQuest runs it live through your connected broker.

2Test

Replay any past session, bar by bar.

The time-travel simulator puts you back on a historical day with the chain and OI as they were, so you can practise the trade before the money is real.

2Test

Backtest across months in one run.

Every backtest becomes a report with entries, win rate and net P&L, so you can compare versions of an idea on evidence.

3Trade

Start from a shape.

Pick a bullish, bearish or neutral structure and it's priced instantly from the live chain. Straddles, strangles, condors, butterflies and more.

3Trade

See the payoff before you place it.

Max profit, max loss, breakevens and probability of profit update as you change strikes, so there are no surprises at expiry.

3Trade

Fire a whole basket at once.

Save groups of orders as baskets and place them together, for yourself or for your team.

3Trade

Every position, one screen.

Live P&L across every account you trade, with one-click square off when it's time to get out.
