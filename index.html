Build it, test it, then trade it.

Every strategy moves through the same three steps, in the same workspace, on the same data.

1

Build

Lay out entries, exits and stops as blocks on a canvas. You can read the logic at a glance, and so can anyone you share it with.

  • Logic blocks. Combine conditions with AND, OR, ANY and IF branches.
  • Actions. Buy, sell, modify, square off or send yourself an alert.
  • Versions. Every save is kept, so you can compare and roll back.

When a strategy is ready, deploy it and DheQuest runs it live through your connected broker.

2

Test

Run the strategy over months of past sessions before any real money is involved, or step through a single day bar by bar.

  • Backtest reports. Entries, win rate and net P&L for every run.
  • Time-travel simulator. Replay any historical session and practise positions as the day unfolds.
  • Paper trading. Keep paper and real results separate, or view them combined.
3

Trade

Price a multi-leg position against the live chain, see the payoff before you send it, then manage everything from one positions screen.

  • Options terminal. Pick a ready-made shape or add legs yourself. See max profit, max loss, breakevens and probability of profit.
  • Baskets. Save a group of orders and place them together.
  • Positions and orders. Live P&L across every account you trade, with one-click square off.
